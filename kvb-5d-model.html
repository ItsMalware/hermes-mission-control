QS Engineering & Architectural Assumptions (From Drawings)
  • Substructure & Foundations: Drilled concrete caissons socketed into Nashville limestone bedrock with thick reinforced concrete mat foundations beneath the dual high-rise elevator/stair cores (1,355 GSF core footprint per floor) and transfer columns.
  • Superstructure Frame Type: Cast-in-place post-tensioned (PT) reinforced concrete flat-plate slabs (8"–10" thick) with high-strength concrete shear walls (8,000–10,000 psi). Acoustic and structural transfer slabs/girders located at Level 5 (parking/ballroom podium to hotel tower step-back), Level 14 (hotel to residential grid transition), and Level 32 (upper tier setback).
  • External Facade System: Unitized aluminum and high-performance low-E glass curtain wall with bronze/terracotta vertical architectural fins on the tower (per Elkus Manfredi rendering), architectural metal louver screening on Podium Parking Levels 1–4, and expansive outdoor Amenity Terraces at Level 5 (hotel pool deck) and Level 38 (residential sky amenity & pool terrace).
  • MEP & Vertical Transportation: Central plant split between Level 13 (Mechanical/Back-of-House transfer floor) and the Level 38M roof mechanical penthouse. Dedicated high-speed traction lift groups serving Hotel (L1–L13), Podium Parking/Ballroom, and Residential Tower (L1–L38).
